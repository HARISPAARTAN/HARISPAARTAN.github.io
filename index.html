04 / research

Master's thesis, explained from zero

My thesis asks whether the scope of positive selection in SelfPatch's patch-level loss changes the label-free object masks that a spectral decoder reads out of a self-supervised Vision Transformer. To understand the method before writing it up, I built an interactive course on the two papers behind it and on the thesis's own rule.

interactive course · nine pageslive

Pixels to Islands: DINO, SelfPatch and my thesis rule

A from-zero course for a reader who has never met a neural network: numbers and vectors, the Vision Transformer, DINO's student and teacher, SelfPatch's patch-level loss, and the contrastive selection rule my thesis studies. Every equation is walked with numbers you can check, every design decision gets a "why" card, and the labs run on real photographs with features from DINO, the released SelfPatch model and my own training runs. Four marks keep facts, real computations, toy simplifications and readings apart.
